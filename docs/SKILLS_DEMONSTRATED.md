# Product Management & FinTech Competencies Demonstrated

**Project**: GroMo FinArva AI Agent Co-Pilot Platform  
**Lead Product Strategist**: **Ayush Shah**  

---

### 1. FinTech Domain Expertise & Ecosystem Mapping
- Mapped India’s ₹8 Lakh Crore financial distribution landscape, identifying the 80% preference for assisted financial sales in Tier 2–4 markets.
- Dissected GroMo's multi-category catalog across Credit Cards (35%), Personal Loans (25%), Insurance (20%), Savings (10%), Demat (5%), and Investments (5%).

### 2. Large-Scale Empirical User Research & NLP Review Mining
- Synthesized insights from **4,000+ GroMo Partner app reviews**, 7 in-person field interviews with semi-urban partners, and 100+ customer phone surveys.
- Identified that **payout delays (36%)** and **unclear product-fit (30%)** accounted for 66% of partner churn, directly informing the product prioritization roadmap.

### 3. AI Agent System Architecture & Workflow Automation
- Designed **TransparAI**, an event-driven autonomous verification agent integrating RabbitMQ queues, Google Cloud Vision OCR, and deterministic regex checks to compress payout auditing from weeks to minutes.
- Architected **GroMoGuide**, an LLM Natural Language-to-SQL multilingual WhatsApp voice/text engine handling 70% of post-sale customer inquiries.

### 4. Product Strategy, Prioritization & North Star Definition
- Evaluated 5 AI modules across GP Earnings Impact, User Adoption, Scalability, Feasibility, and Business Impact to select **AI Retention Engine as MVP** followed by **FinFit Advisor**.
- Defined the North Star Metric: **Percentage of GP Sales Influenced by AI Recommendations**, serving as a leading indicator for platform GMV and agent retention.

### 5. Phased Product Roadmapping & Risk Mitigation
- Engineered a 12-month, 4-phase rollout plan aligning telemetry tracking, OCR integration, multilingual LLM fine-tuning, and behavioral habit modeling.
- Formulated proactive mitigations including "Explain Decision" transparency modals, conversational micro-forms, and human fallback escalation rules.
