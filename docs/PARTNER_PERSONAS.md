# GroMo Partner & End-Customer Personas

**Project**: FinArva AI — Reimagining the FinAI Agent Experience  
**Author**: Ayush Shah

## 1. GroMo Partner (GP) Segments

### Persona 1: Ashish (20) — College Student GP
- **Demographics**: 2nd-year B.Com student in tier-2 city (Bhopal).
- **Behavior**: Shares referral links casually on WhatsApp college groups; works in quick 15-minute bursts between classes.
- **Mindset**: Wants risk-free supplemental income; expects immediate feedback.
- **Pain Point**: Small initial network; motivation plunges when bank partner verification delays payouts by 10+ days.
- **FinArva Value**: TransparAI live tracker eliminates anxiety; FinFit advisor crafts custom pitches for young peers.

### Persona 2: Ajay (35) — Clothing Shopkeeper GP
- **Demographics**: Retail merchant with steady daily walk-in traffic in suburban Kanpur.
- **Behavior**: Actively shares financial links with customers and relatives but lacks clarity on what to pitch.
- **Mindset**: Leverage high footfall and trust capital to build a secondary revenue stream.
- **Pain Point**: Shoppers hesitate taking financial advice from a cloth merchant; fears tarnishing shop reputation with bad product suggestions.
- **FinArva Value**: FinFit Advisor provides institutional-grade credit card and loan recommendations tailored to the shopper's verified profile.

### Persona 3: Neha (28) — Full-Time Financial Advisor GP
- **Demographics**: Experienced independent agent managing 20+ active clients daily in Jaipur.
- **Behavior**: Works full-time; uses structured CRM and daily follow-ups.
- **Mindset**: Ambitious, target-driven; aspires to be in GroMo's top 1% earners.
- **Pain Point**: Drowned in post-sale support inquiries ("Where is my policy document?", "Why didn't my auto-debit process?"), leaving zero time to source new high-ticket insurance leads.
- **FinArva Value**: GroMoGuide WhatsApp Voice Bot resolves 70% of post-sale queries automatically; AI Retention Engine flags renewal dates.

## 2. End-Customer Personas (Tier 2/3/4 Bharat)

### Rajesh Modi (42, Tier-3 Hardware Merchant)
- **Mindset**: Financially conservative; deeply distrusts faceless mobile apps with bank details.
- **Needs**: Simple Hindi explanations, transparent loan terms, and a local human contact person.

### Pooja Sharma (31, Homemaker & New Digital Banking User)
- **Mindset**: First-time digital financial product buyer; seeks health insurance protection for her family.
- **Needs**: Regional language voice support, absence of confusing English financial jargon, assurance of data privacy.
